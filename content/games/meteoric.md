Meteoric is an educational game made for the open university. The premise of the game is that you have to 
select meteors and direct them towards a planet, cratering the planet as efficiently as possible. This game teaches 
how different characteristics of a meteor such as mass, velocity, etc. affect the resultant crater. 

https://www.open.edu/openlearn/science-maths-technology/science/physics-and-astronomy/meteoric
