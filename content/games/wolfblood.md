Wolfblood shadow runner is a unity app made for the CBBC show **Wolfblood**. The game has two main parts – an endless 
runner mode and a story mode. The gameplay is similar to other endless runners such as subway surfers or temple run. 
In this game you play as the wolf and swipe to avoid obstacles. You upgrade the wolf as you progress. 
My responsibilities in this game were creating editor tools for the cutscenes and writing an api that allowed 
8-bit sprites to be loaded at runtime, decreasing filesize.
