Official description: Horse Racing Solitaire is a feel-good mash-up of solitaire and horse racing. Play as an up-and-coming jockey, clear card combos to build speed, and outpace your opponents in the final stretch to win! Collect, train, and breed a stable of charming horses, unlock new tracks around the world, and renovate a homestead ranch as you climb the ranks.

I initially joined Salto Games as Technical Director, converting the core logic to use Metaplay. Overseeing the outsourcing team and freelancers as well as making key contributions to the game, ensuring the architecture is as scalable and stable as is required.
